Buyer beware at DCH Toyota Wappingers

Ask about the 9 year warranty that is exactly the same as the 7 year warranty, for $500 less.

In May 2026 I bought a Toyota Gold Certified 2022 Toyota Tundra from DCH Toyota in Wappingers Falls. Working with this sales team, specifically the sales manager, Daryl Callahan, has been my worst car buying experience ever. I have had to spend dozens of hours and awkward conversations to get what I paid for.

Did I mention that the truck I bought was a trade-in from the sales manager, Daryl Callahan? He was aware of many of these issues, and they still were not fixed before the truck was sold, even though they fall outside what a Gold Certified vehicle should be.

I have been left with no other choice but to present my issues to the internet. Below you can see every issue I have had and decide for yourself whether you would want to do business with a place that puts a person like Daryl Callahan in charge of the sales department. If nothing else, make sure you ask about the 9 year warranty that is exactly the same as the 7 year warranty they offer, for $500 less. They will not tell you about it unless you ask.

ISSUE 01

Toyota's 160-point inspection not done by a technician, many issues missed

Summary

Part of the Gold Certified process is a detailed inspection of every car, signed off by several people, to make sure it is up to Toyota's standards. Toyota's official 160-Point Quality Assurance Inspection Check Sheet requires three signatures: a "Required Technician Signature" on a line labeled "Toyota Trained Technician," a "Required Manager Signature," and a Used Car Manager. The dealership's own website says "A rigorous 160-point inspection is completed on every gold certified Toyota" (their program page).

The checklist I was provided shows all three lines signed by the same person. The dealership's own staff page lists that person as a Sales Manager, not a technician. Even though the form requires the signee to confirm they are a technician.

According to this document, a technician never looked at my truck before it was Gold Certified. This is particularly troubling considering every box was marked as passing, even though I had many issues with the vehicle, including one that was unsafe. I have seen this pattern of neglecting advertised protocols when they are inconvenient throughout my dealings with this dealership.

ISSUE 03

The hands-free tailgate drop does not work from the key fob or the side button. Manager misleading about function.

Summary

I told Daryl Callahan, the General Sales Manager at DCH Wappingers Falls Toyota, that the rear tailgate does not drop with the Toyota brand tonneau cover installed. Rather than investigate, he told me the button only unlocks the latch, so it is working correctly.

This is incorrect. The fob button itself shows the tailgate down, and Toyota's Owner's Manual says: "Press and hold the switch. Tailgate opens by gravity."

I told him that if he could show me documentation that the Toyota cover is supposed to stop the gate from dropping, I would drop the issue. He could not point me to anything, so I did the research myself. Toyota's build sheet shows this cover is a genuine Toyota part, sold new on this truck by DCH Wappingers Falls Toyota itself. Toyota's own installation instructions for it end with a check the installer must complete: "Tailgate should open and close without interference." Mine does not. The dealership still will not acknowledge this as an issue and maintains it works as intended.

This is one example. I have been intentionally misled many times, to the point where I no longer feel comfortable working with this man.
